12:30 PM New in 0.5.3

Cancel your Granola and Wispr Flow subscriptions

Meet the Stapler: a small round window that floats over every app. It takes dictation, writes down your meetings and hands both to your agents. Transcription runs locally, so your voice never leaves the machine.

Dictate into any app. Hold Option on a Mac, or Control+Alt+Space on Windows and Linux, and talk. Let go and the words land in whatever text field is in front of you.
Record the meeting, both sides. Your microphone is written down as You and the call as Them, on Mac, Windows and Linux. Fix the transcript and send it to any agent.
Screenshot it. Drag a box, add a line, send it to an agent. The picture stays on your disk.
Record a message. Talk for up to five minutes, read it back, pick who gets it.
Leave invisible. One click and it drops out of every screen share and recording.

The Stapler is part of Pro. Dictation in the Munder Difflin message box works in the free version too. It understands 126 technical names out of the box, plus your own.
