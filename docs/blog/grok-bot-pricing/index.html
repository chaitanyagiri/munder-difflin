Grok Bot Pricing: What $20, $30, $100 and $300 a Month Actually Buy

Grok Bot has no plan of its own. It comes bundled with SuperGrok and Cursor plans from $20 to $300 a month. Here is what each one gets you, what the free trial is, and the $0 route for work agents.

CG Chaitanya Giri · Sep 26, 2026 · 4 min read Comparisons
TL;DR

The cheapest way into Grok Bot is Cursor Pro at $20 a month. The cheapest SpaceXAI plan with it is SuperGrok at $30. The biggest agent allowance sits in SuperGrok Heavy at $300. There is a seven day trial, not a free plan. If you only need work agents, you can run them free on your own computer.

Grok Bot is SpaceXAI’s team of always on AI agents, called Bots. They sign into your apps, do the work on a cloud computer and keep going with your laptop shut. It reached 418,000 weekly users a month after launch. The pricing confuses a lot of people, because there is no “Grok Bot plan” to buy. It comes inside other subscriptions.

If what you want is agents for your work, there is also a way to pay nothing for the app. Munder Difflin runs a team of agents on your own computer using AI engines you already have. It is free and open source. We compare the costs at the end.

Every plan that includes Grok Bot #

Grok Bot launched in beta on 11 August 2026 for only three top plans: SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra and Cursor Premium Teams. On 26 August SpaceXAI opened it to more plans. Here is where it stands now.

Is Grok Bot free? #

No. There is a free trial, and Cursor’s Grok Bot plans page describes it as a usage credit with a seven day window. It does not turn into a paid plan by itself. When the credit or the week runs out, you need one of the plans above.

Does Grok Bot eat into your plan’s usage? #

No, and this is the best part of the pricing. SpaceXAI says Grok Bot comes with its own usage, separate from your Grok and Cursor plans, so a job you hand to a Bot does not count against your chat or coding allowance. Bot usage resets every week. Bigger plans get a bigger weekly allowance, and SpaceXAI has not published how many tasks each allowance covers.

At launch, eesel’s review noted there was no spend cap on extra usage. If you turn on paid extra usage, keep an eye on it.

What you get for the money #

Every plan gets the same product: Bots that work on a cloud computer, sign into your apps through a browser, run routines on a schedule and hand work to each other in shared threads. The desktop app runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, and there are phone apps.

What you do not get at any price:

  • A choice of model. Cursor picks the model and the mix can change.
  • Your own computer. Bots run only on Cursor’s cloud computers in the US. Running them on your own servers is not supported.
  • Walls between Bots. All the Bots on your account share one computer and its logins.

We cover what those limits mean in our Grok Bot alternatives guide.

Which plan should you pick? #

  • You already pay for Cursor Pro. You already have Grok Bot. Try it before paying for anything else.
  • You want Grok Bot and you use Grok for chat. SuperGrok at $30 covers both.
  • You will run several Bots all day. Look at SuperGrok Heavy or Cursor Ultra, because the weekly allowance is what runs out first.
  • You are buying for a team. Cursor Teams plans, priced per seat.

The $0 route for work agents #

If your Bots would mostly do engineering or other work on files, compare the cost of running the agents yourself.

Munder Difflin is a free and open source desktop app (MIT licence) for macOS, Windows and Linux. It turns the AI tools you already use into a team: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, OpenCode, Grok’s own CLI and six others. Each agent gets a desk, an inbox, long term memory and a shared task board, and you can start work from Slack or a webhook.

The honest trade: agents run only while your computer is on, and Munder Difflin will not do errands like booking travel. For overnight work, a Mac mini that stays awake does the job for a one time cost.

FAQ

How much does Grok Bot cost?

Grok Bot has no standalone subscription. Since 26 August 2026 it comes with SuperGrok ($30 a month), SuperGrok Plus ($100), SuperGrok Heavy ($300) and every paid Cursor plan, from Cursor Pro at $20 a month up to Cursor Ultra at $200. Teams get it with Cursor Teams plans, which cost $120 a seat on Premium Teams.

Is Grok Bot free?

Only as a trial. Cursor describes the free trial as a usage credit with a seven day window, and it does not turn into a paid plan on its own. After that you need one of the paid plans.

What is the cheapest way to get Grok Bot?

Cursor Pro at $20 a month, which has included Grok Bot since 26 August 2026. The cheapest SpaceXAI plan with Grok Bot is SuperGrok at $30 a month; SuperGrok Lite does not include it.

Does Grok Bot use up my Cursor or SuperGrok usage?

No. SpaceXAI says Grok Bot comes with its own usage, separate from your Grok and Cursor plans, so work you hand to a Bot does not count against your existing allowance. Bot usage resets weekly.

Is there a free alternative to Grok Bot?

For work agents, Munder Difflin is free and open source. It runs a team of agents on your own computer with engines you already pay for, or with a free local model. OpenClaw and Hermes Agent are free, open source personal assistants.
