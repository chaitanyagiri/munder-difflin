For a product that's an office homage, we were weirdly hard to hang out with. Feedback lived
in GitHub issues, launch threads on
[Reddit](/blog/what-reddit-told-us-about-munder-difflin/) and
[Product Hunt](/blog/number-five-on-product-hunt/), and an inbox we won't discuss. Launch week
made the gap obvious: between "formal bug report" and "shouting into a Reddit thread" there
was nowhere to just… ask a quick question. Or post a screenshot of nine agents pulling an
all-nighter.

So: **[discord.gg/SEDzP5ZPk5](https://discord.gg/SEDzP5ZPk5)**. The lobby is open.

## What it's for

**Release news, first.** We just shipped
[seven releases in eight days](/blog/seven-releases-in-eight-days/); Discord is where drops
get announced the moment they're tagged, and where we'll be honest about what's still rough.

**Setup help, fast.** A meaningful share of launch-week frustration was
[first-run problems](/blog/launching-munder-difflin-v0-4-4/) that a two-minute conversation
would have solved. If onboarding fights you, come fight back in a channel where someone's
awake — the community spans enough timezones now that someone usually is.

**Show your floor.** The screenshots people posted during launch week — offices mid-swarm,
agents [working through the night](/blog/claude-code-automation-while-you-sleep/) — were the
best part of the whole launch. They deserve a wall. This is the wall.

## Employee of the month

Here's the part we're irrationally proud of. Contributing to Munder Difflin now comes with a
title: **merge a pull request, and a GitHub workflow automatically grants you the *employee of
the month* role in the Discord.** The PR template has an optional `Discord:` field so the bot
knows who to celebrate. No nomination committee, no monthly limit, no plaque in a hallway —
just a workflow that fires on merge, because in this office, recognition is automated too.

The inaugural class earned it properly:
[v0.4.4 carries nine community PRs](/blog/launching-munder-difflin-v0-4-4/) —
[@gts-47](https://github.com/gts-47) with eight, from atomic task-ledger writes to un-silencing
an agent's wake nudge, and [@baziyer](https://github.com/baziyer) with the fix that stops the
office floor rendering when nobody's watching it. If you've been thinking about a first
contribution, the [good-first-issue path](https://github.com/chaitanyagiri/munder-difflin)
now ends with a job title.

{% img "note-2", "Merge a PR, get the role. Recognition is a workflow now, like everything else around here." %}

## The rules of the room

Short version: GitHub remains the source of truth — bugs and feature requests belong in
issues, where they [get triaged into real work](/blog/turn-feedback-into-a-backlog-with-agents/)
rather than scrolling away. The Discord is for everything that's conversation-shaped. Be kind,
assume good faith, and if you built something weird with your hive, we absolutely want to see
it.

The product has always been a bet that work is better with
[a floor full of colleagues you can see](/blog/building-an-ai-office-floor/). It was overdue
that the humans got a floor too. Come say hi:
**[discord.gg/SEDzP5ZPk5](https://discord.gg/SEDzP5ZPk5)**.
